# MeowChan 1.0 🐾 — Your offline archive, upgraded

**More chans. Better reading. A whole lot more control.**

MeowChan 1.0 is here! This is a big update: a major desktop UI rework, **11 imageboard
sources**, **five local media filters**, and new ways to find, read, organize and
clean up your collection. There is plenty to explore whether you want a compact
downloader or a full offline reading workspace.

**Before upgrading, back up your whole profile and media.** See the
[upgrade and fallback notes](#upgrading--and-what-to-do-if-something-breaks) below.

[**Download 1.0.0 →**](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

## The good stuff

- **A fresh workspace that still feels like MeowChan.** The Semi + Ursa UI
  rework brings clearer layouts, roomier controls, better spacing and more
  consistent dialogs across Downloads, Archive, Settings and Plugins. Keep it
  compact in **Barebones**, or bring more controls into view with **Simple** and
  **Advanced**. Settings now has **eight focused categories**, with drafts kept as
  you switch pages. Light and Dark, English and German are built in.
- **Eleven sources. One collection.** The **16 supplied plugins** include eleven
  sources and five local filters. Alongside 4chan and 8kun, the source plugins
  cover **Lainchan, Kohlchan, Wizchan, Endchan, 2ch, Leftypol, Uboachan, ZZZchan
  and SushiChan**. The plugin overview puts descriptions and everyday
  actions first. Want to tinker? **14 editable C# examples** and the bundled
  compiler let you get started without installing the .NET SDK. Experimental
  sources are clearly marked and start disabled; see the permission notes below.
- **Keep the media you actually want.** Choose from dimensions, file-size and
  original-name rules, a **SHA-256 blocklist**, **animation/actual-format checks**
  and **PNG metadata-field detection**. Filters work on locally staged files
  before they enter the archive. One filter applies per effective rule.
- **Make time for the reading.** Save named **reader groups**, navigate captured
  posts with the keyboard, and use optional **Continue reading** to pick up at
  your saved place through the topbar book button. Keep post bookmarks and
  notes, and make the app's shortcuts your own.
- **Find it again.** Combine source, board, state and date filters, then save
  searches you use often. Star threads and media, rate media, and return to the
  parts of your archive you care about.
- **Less clutter, more collection.** **Clean up archive…** previews threads with
  **0–2 saved media versions** while protecting favorites, ratings, bookmarks,
  notes, reader groups, reading positions and entries still in use. Review the
  losses and choose what to delete. Archive counts now reflect stored media
  rather than an empty current download job.
- **Give the media some room.** The gallery follows your theme and offers an
  optional **media focus** view that folds away tools and details while keeping
  navigation close at hand. Video gets a compact **Play/Pause, seek, audio and
  repeat** strip. Repeat applies to the current medium; audio controls explain
  when a track or usable output is unavailable.

Try a reader group, save your favorite archive search, or finally clear out the
threads you never meant to keep. Portable HTML exports remain a way to take your
saved posts and verified originals with you.

<details>
<summary><strong>Reading, filters and archive counts: the details</strong></summary>

Reader groups contain manually selected threads and do not change watches or
downloads. **Continue reading** is optional: enable it in Settings to remember
one explicitly read place per profile. Archive date filters use local
registration dates. Rule templates copy selected values into an editable draft;
saving remains explicit. Filters do not chain or automatically tag media.

The **Saved versions** column counts archived media versions across a thread's
recorded media folders, independently of the current download job and media
filters. Missing files and files whose length differs from the manifest are not
counted; unreadable storage appears as unavailable. This count checks presence
and length; opening media still verifies its checksum.

Cleanup previews threads with 0–2 verified saved media versions. Unknown storage
is excluded. Review the current losses and explicitly confirm permanent local
deletion. The first full inventory can take time on a large archive; back up
before deleting.

Plugin enable/disable applies to the selected valid build immediately; saved
settings and candidate choices apply at the next start. See the
[first-session guide](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/blob/v1.0.0/docs/plugins/using-plugins.md#first-session)
and [filter guide](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/blob/v1.0.0/docs/plugins/local-media-filters.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Experimental sources and integrity permissions</strong></summary>

**Kohlchan, Wizchan, Endchan, 2ch, Leftypol, Uboachan, ZZZchan and SushiChan
are Experimental.** These sources start disabled when first registered in a
profile. Existing profiles retain saved activation and permission choices. The
Experimental label itself grants no exception.

For **2ch, Uboachan, ZZZchan and SushiChan**, submitting a matching URL offers
activation with strict integrity checks. These four sources have no
provider-mismatch permission. Uboachan's supplied per-attachment SHA-1 values
are verified. Approximate provider sizes remain separate raw hints rather than
invented exact byte expectations. 2ch original redirects are checked against
explicitly declared original-media hosts at every hop.

For **Kohlchan, Wizchan, Endchan and Leftypol**, provider size or MD5 can differ
from delivered bytes. **Activate and allow differences**
(**Aktivieren und Abweichungen erlauben**) explicitly activates the source and
permits provider size/MD5 differences for the current profile, plugin owner and
source. Cancel grants nothing. Activation and permission are saved separately:
existing watches may resume after activation, while submitted URLs wait for both
steps to succeed. If permission saving fails, activation may already be saved;
review the state and retry explicitly. Closing does not undo saved activation.

Use **Sources** to review or revoke **Allow provider size and MD5 mismatches**.
Manual **Enable plugin** grants no exception; a later URL for an active source
does not restore a revoked permission. Retry previous failed media explicitly
through **Downloads → thread details → Retry missing media**; granting permission
does not automatically retry old failures.

Provider metadata and measured local values stay separate. Supplied provider
SHA-1/SHA-256, local verification, complete transfers, actual byte/pixel/storage
limits, filters and cancellation remain enforced. Revoking permission blocks new
acceptance that needs it; previously accepted archive media remains readable
when its local length and SHA-256 verify. New attachment revisions need their
own valid binding. See the
[permission guide](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/blob/v1.0.0/docs/plugins/using-plugins.md#experimental-sources-and-media-checks).

</details>

## Upgrading — and what to do if something breaks

**Please report bugs as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/issues/new).**
If 1.0 gives you trouble, [0.9.0 is still available](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/releases/tag/v0.9.0).
Use an untouched profile or a pre-upgrade backup when going back.

Close every older writer before upgrading. Back up the whole profile and media,
and keep exports separately. Do not let 0.9.0 and 1.0 write to the same data
simultaneously. Trying a separate profile (`MEOWCHAN_PROFILE`) and media directory
first is useful.

1.0 writes **archive schema 5 and media manifest 4**. Older records remain
readable in 1.0, including bindings without the new optional evidence. There is
no supported downgrade of a profile changed by 1.0: to try 0.9.0 after a problem,
use an untouched profile or restore your pre-upgrade backup to a separate
location. Keep the 1.0 data available when reporting the issue.

<details>
<summary><strong>For plugin and library authors</strong></summary>

The Library, PluginSdk and optional PluginHost package version is `1.0.0`; the
independent plugin SDK protocol contract remains `1.0.0`. Their public APIs are
**not stable throughout 1.x**. Plugin authors should use the exact delivered SDK
package and consult the
[author guide](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/blob/v1.0.0/docs/plugins/README.md).
Local C# plugins run with user permissions; worker separation is not an OS sandbox.

Custom library providers must explicitly bind new captured attachment revisions
to the acquisition committed by the media store. Downloading without references
and later capturing matching metadata no longer creates an implicit association.
Update the provider logic and rebuild; recompilation alone does not migrate that
behavior. See the
[migration note](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/blob/v1.0.0/CHANGELOG.md#breaking-librarysdk)
and [package consumer example](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/blob/v1.0.0/samples/LibMeowChan.Consumer/README.md).

</details>

## Known limits

- Automated and hosted native checks apply to their recorded source and
  environment. Everyday testing is not comprehensive across every platform and
  device; physical audio/GPU/FUSE behavior and complete screen-reader/IME coverage
  remain limited.
- Source reachability and future provider behavior are not guaranteed. Live
  checks use bounded samples; they do not establish availability of every board,
  thread or attachment, or prove the causes of every historical metadata conflict.
- Plugin-supplied names and parameter labels can remain in English; the host
  interface follows its selected language.
- macOS packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
- Automatic settings-backup recovery disables recovered experimental-source
  exceptions until you explicitly grant them again. Valid primary settings keep
  their saved choices.

## Report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/grassmann-it/MeowChan/issues/new) with the exact
version/package, OS/CPU, display scaling, view mode, reproduction steps, expected
and actual behavior. Mention whether the profile is fresh or upgraded and which
plugins are enabled. Relevant logs and screenshots help. If you try 0.9.0 as a
fallback, use a separate untouched profile or pre-upgrade backup and include
whether the problem also occurs there.
