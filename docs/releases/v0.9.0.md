# MeowChan 0.9.0 — the UI relaunch is here 🐾

**New name. New desktop. Welcome to MeowChan.** 🐾

0.9.0 is the **first release under the MeowChan name** and our biggest desktop relaunch yet: a redesigned Avalonia interface for Windows, macOS and Linux, a new cat identity, dark and light themes, and a connected offline experience for downloading, reading and browsing your archive.

From the full-window thread reader to the image/video gallery, from board-filter previews to portable HTML collections, this release makes saved threads feel at home on your desktop. **Barebones stays the simple default** for new profiles. Want more? Simple and Advanced reveal more detail without interrupting your downloads.

Thank you to everyone who tested, reported rough edges and helped shape this release. Time to give your archive a new home. 🎉

## Highlights

- **Make the workspace yours.** Barebones, Simple and Advanced each remember their own table columns, order and widths, archive layout, density and reading size. Downloads, Archive, Sources, Rules, Diagnostics and Settings share one workspace; navigation keeps your input, selection and running downloads. Click the cat in the header to open About.
- **Read and browse offline.** Search saved threads, captured posts and media; follow local quotes and backlinks; and move between the full-window reader and shared image/video gallery. Gallery navigation offers Fit/1:1, zoom, pan and saved-post context. Fullscreen works across the workspace, reader and gallery. Closing the gallery returns to the saved post associated with the media currently shown, while list and search origins keep their place.
- **Act on several threads at once.** Multi-select watches or archived threads to copy links, remove watches or export selected threads as one portable HTML collection. Favorites retain exact saved-media revisions, and verified originals can be exported without downloading again. Permanent archive deletion shows the local files to be removed and asks for confirmation; ordinary Delete keeps the archive.
- **Control what gets saved.** A board-admission wizard previews discovered threads, title/opening-post filters and individual choices before adding a batch. Inherited media rules, persistent Pause/Resume, bounded missing-media retries, manual repair and Diagnostics make downloads easier to manage. Repeat HTML exports can update a recognized earlier result, with backup protection for changed or reduced output.
- **Use the desktop your way.** Dark, Light and System themes; built-in English and German; optional JSON language packs; keyboard help; and separate Close to tray and Minimize to tray settings are available.

MeowChan watches supported 4chan and 8kun threads and boards, saves selected original media and can capture posts for offline reading. See the [user guide](../Help.md) for controls, rules, export, retry and upgrade details.

## Upgrading

Back up your profile and media directories before upgrading. Existing watches, settings and assigned media paths remain usable, but do not run an older application against the same profile or media destination concurrently. New profiles use `.MeowChan` and `Downloads/MeowChan`; a sole existing legacy profile is used in place. If both profile directories exist, select one with `MEOWCHAN_PROFILE`. New profile and media formats do not support downgrading.

The reusable library is renamed **`libmeowchan`** with `MeowChan.*` namespaces. Consumers must update package, assembly and namespace references; binary compatibility and an automatic NuGet redirect are not provided. The .NET 10 baseline remains.
